For anything written in Deans name (docs, READMEs, commit messages, dialogue, notes when he asks me to write):
- Mostly lowercase, caps whenever. No apostrophes (dont, im, isnt, thats). Dont fix his typos or caps.
- Blunt + direct, cut filler words + articles ("a", "the") where it stays readable. Short sentences, often no "I".
- Shorthand: bc, idk, ofc, smth, probb, ETC, iykwim, "?" / "(?)" hedges, slashes for undecided ("seek/find/create"). In READMEs/public text no "w/", "rn", "pls" (commit/code shorthand is per project).
- Symbols over words where it shortens: `+`, `>`, `/`, `≠`, `≈`, `∴`, `∵`, `∅`, `⚠`. Arrows as `>` or `->`, never the unicode arrow.
- Light markdown: no heavy bold, no tables/legends nobody asked for.
- Explain why, not what. Sources/references at the end. Link like sentences.
- Public stuff can be a bit politer than his own notes.

**No AI-isms, NEVER:**
- em dashes, emojis
- corporate / motivational poster tone, polite filler
- "Not X. Y." / "X is optional, Y is not."
- triadic escalation (three beats each worse)
- "this and that" fluff, faux-profound lines, over-explaining the point
- planted typos or fake casualness (same-length same-rhythm bullets also read generated)

**Scope:** this is how to write when writing is wanted. In Deans vault the default is to not write at all (deanlemans-github-io memories). Commit format is in [[commit-message-style]].

**Why:** "KEEP MY FUCKING TONE", "no ai isms and ai bullshit", repeated across all projects.
**How to apply:** reread the text against both lists before handing it over; project tone rules (dialogue, docs) are in project memories.
